Questions

What will Relink cost?

The waitlist hears first, with an early-bird price that ends at launch.

When do I get my invite?

Invites go out in small waves, earliest signups first. Your invite comes by email.

Does Relink send messages for me?

Never. Relink opens the conversation on LinkedIn, and you press send.

Does Relink automate anything on LinkedIn?

No. It doesn't send, connect, like or visit profiles. It only reads your own inbox from your signed-in browser, with human-paced pauses.

Where does my data go?

Your conversations go to your Relink account and nowhere else. Relink never marks a message as read.

What do I need to use it?

Chrome on a desktop or laptop, and your LinkedIn account. Relink runs in the browser, and a Chrome extension syncs your inbox.

Is Relink made by LinkedIn?

No. Relink is independent and not affiliated with LinkedIn.
