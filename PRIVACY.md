# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 7 November 2026_

LinkedIn Comment Streak counts how many comments you post on LinkedIn each day and shows the totals to you.

**What it reads.** On linkedin.com, it checks the web address, method and status of LinkedIn's own network requests to tell when a comment has been posted. It never reads the text of your comments, your messages, your profile or anyone else's data.

**What it stores.** A count per day (for example, `2026-11-07: 4`), your daily goal and your chime setting. These live only in Chrome's local extension storage on your computer.

**What it sends.** Nothing. It has no server, no analytics and no tracking, and it shares no data with anyone.

**Deleting your data.** Removing the extension deletes everything it stored.

**Contact.** Open an issue at https://github.com/authenticryan/linkedin-comment-streak/issues.

This extension is not affiliated with or endorsed by LinkedIn.
