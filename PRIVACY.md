# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 12 November 2026_

Comment Streak for LinkedIn counts how many comments you post on LinkedIn each day and shows the totals to you.

**What it reads.** On linkedin.com, it checks the web address, method and status of LinkedIn's own network requests to tell when a comment has been posted. It never reads the text of your comments, your messages, your profile or anyone else's data.

**Without an account (the default).** Everything stays in Chrome's local extension storage on your computer: a count per day, your daily goal and your settings. Nothing is sent anywhere.

**With backup and sync (optional).** If you sign in, the extension sends these to the Comment Streak sync server:

- your email address, to sign you in with a one-time code
- your comment count per day for each of your computers, and your daily goal
- whether you want occasional product-update emails (off unless you tick the box)

It never sends the text of your comments, the posts you comment on, or anything else from LinkedIn.

**Who processes it.** The sync server runs on Vercel, the data is stored in a Neon Postgres database, and sign-in emails are sent by Resend. They process the data only to run this service.

**Emails.** You get a sign-in code when you ask for one. You get product updates only if you ticked the box, and you can untick it in the extension at any time.

**Sharing.** Your data is not sold or shared. There are no analytics and no tracking.

**Deleting your data.** In the extension, open **Back up & sync** and click **Delete my account and synced data**. This deletes your account and all of your synced counts from the server at once. Removing the extension deletes the data on your computer.

**Contact.** Open an issue at https://github.com/authenticryan/linkedin-comment-streak/issues.

This extension is not affiliated with or endorsed by LinkedIn.
